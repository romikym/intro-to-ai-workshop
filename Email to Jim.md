# Email to Jim

**Subject:** Walkthrough of the AI workshop deck — what it does and how to drive it

---

Hey Jim,

Quick rundown of everything the deck can do before Tuesday so you're not seeing any of it for the first time on stage. Most of it is invisible until you need it, which is the point — but a few things are worth knowing in advance.

**The deck itself**

It's at https://eloquent-sunshine-4f89c7.netlify.app. Eleven slides total: title, intro, what AI actually is, workforce readiness, beyond AI, deployment strategy, AI for small business, what AI can do, the major models, start-this-week plan, and the Q&A / take-home slide at the end. Arrow keys move forward and back, `G` opens a grid overview of all slides, `B` blacks out the screen mid-talk, and `F` goes fullscreen. The nav chrome at the bottom mirrors all of that with buttons if you'd rather click.

**Speaker notes pop-out**

This is the one I'd really like you to try ahead of time. Click the document icon in the bottom-right chrome and it opens a separate browser window — drag it to your phone or a second screen. It shows the current slide's notes, a thumbnail of the next slide, and you can edit notes inline (they sync back to the deck). Font size and thumbnail size are adjustable. Arrow keys in that window also drive the deck, so you can navigate slides from your phone while looking at the audience. It stays awake automatically (Wake Lock), so your phone won't dim mid-talk.

**The Q&A slide is where most of the magic lives**

Three things happen on slide 11:

The big QR code on the right takes audience phones straight to a question form. They type a question, hit submit, and it appears in a live queue panel in the bottom-right of the slide (visible only to us — hidden on mobile). When you click a question in that queue, it opens a centered "theater mode" card that shows the question in big italic type and streams Claude's answer live in front of the room. There's a matrix-style decode effect on the text — feels theatrical without being cheesy. Hit Esc or click Done when you're finished and the question is marked answered and disappears from the queue.

The "Chat with Claude" button on the same slide opens a multi-turn chat modal where you can have a back-and-forth with Claude in front of the audience — useful if a question turns into an exploration. It streams in real time, you can stop generation mid-answer, and "New chat" wipes the slate.

**Take-home for the audience**

Every audience phone hits a different UI — a single scrolling page instead of the slide carousel. Optimized for speed (no fancy backgrounds, GPU-only animations). Includes the same Ask + Claude buttons in a sticky bar at the bottom so they can keep submitting questions throughout the talk, not just at the end. They can also tap our presenter cards to save our contact info directly to their phone (vCard download — works on iPhone and Android).

**A few small things worth knowing**

The presenter cheat-sheet popup (lightning bolt icon in the chrome) has section-by-section talking points if you blank on a transition. Esc closes any open overlay. The deck only runs in dark mode — I disabled the light toggle since the projector washes it out. If anything ever looks broken visually, the reload button in the chrome forces a fresh asset load.

If anything's confusing or you want to walk through the Q&A flow live before Tuesday, let me know and I can do a 10-min screen share.

— Romik
